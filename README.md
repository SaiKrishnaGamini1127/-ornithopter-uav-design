# Ornithopter: Radio-Controlled Flapping-Wing Aircraft

Major project, B.Tech. Mechanical Engineering, Sreenidhi Institute of
Science and Technology (2021-22).

Group project by G. Abhiram, Sai Krishna G, and Syed Sameer.
Guide: Dr. G. Konda Reddy, Department of Mechanical Engineering, SNIST.

## The aircraft
A flapping-wing aircraft inspired by bird flight, controlled by radio.
- Wingspan 1200 mm, chord 350 mm, total mass 370 g
- Carbon fiber spars with an EPP membrane
- BLDC motor with a 1:37.5 gear reduction (designed for about 6 Hz flapping)
- Radio control with speed controller, two servos, and a 2S LiPo battery

## My part
- Modeled the wings in SolidWorks
- Set up the electronics
- Helped assemble the aircraft
- Flew the aircraft during flight testing
